A note for parents

More than a song. A moment together.

Some of the best dance parties happen in the kitchen. Put on a song, make up a move, and see who joins in. Nobody has to get it right.

Make up a move

Try a twirl, a stomp, or whatever your feet come up with.

Get a little silly

Wiggle your arms like tentacles or see who can freeze in the funniest pose.

Sing it again

When they ask to hear it one more time, you might catch yourself singing along, too.

About Joyful Songhouse

Joyful Songhouse is all about creating fun, catchy songs for kids to listen to, sing along with, and dance to (without driving parents crazy when they’re requested 100 times a day).

Created by a working mom and Berklee College of Music alum who wanted to find a way to combine her love for music and creativity with spending more time creating alongside her own children.

“I’m no illustrator, so I wanted to find a way to make fun videos that kids could enjoy without being overly stimulating. My hope is that the videos might even spark some creativity and inspire little ones to try recreating what they see themselves!”

Thanks for being here and being part of the journey!
