Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Joyful Songhouse is a place for families to find songs and dance along together. You can browse the site without an account. The site has no contact form or newsletter signup.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use that information to reply and handle your request.

A note for parents and caregivers

We ask that an adult email us on a child’s behalf. Please leave out children’s names, photos, and other personal details. If you think your child sent us personal information, email us so we can look into it. You can also ask us to review or delete personal information about your child.

Services used by the site

Netlify hosts the site. Your browser sends technical information, such as its IP address, when it requests a page. Your browser also requests the site’s typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives technical information needed to serve the fonts.

Our links to YouTube and Instagram take you to those services. Their privacy policies explain how they handle information when you visit them.

Questions about privacy

If you have a privacy question or request, email us at Hello@joyfulsonghouse.com.
